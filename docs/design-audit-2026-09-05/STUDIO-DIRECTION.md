# Reference-inspired Nexoris presentation

References reviewed on 5 September 2026: [Wolfpack Digital](https://www.wolfpack-digital.com/) and [Cuberto](https://cuberto.com/). Both homepages were read and inspected visually in the browser. Their branding, images, client claims, awards, testimonials, and copy have not been imported into Nexoris.

## Applied principles

Cuberto's restrained hierarchy informed larger typography, a clearer opening proposition, and generous space around imagery. Wolfpack's emphasis on work and delivery informed a stronger presentation of Nexoris's real in-house products and practical workflows.

The Nexoris homepage retains its original ProductMockup and supplied image. The existing AI/ownership sentence moves below the visual, so the opening paragraph is easier to scan without losing the information. The four existing service groups become numbered editorial rows, with all links preserved. Problem, industry and value sections use quieter borders and more readable headings instead of repeated elevated cards.

The product showcase uses the existing Covyvo and GLEEN assets and clearly identifies both as in-house products under active development. Links lead to their existing descriptions on the case-study hub. Client case studies still come from CMS; these product cards do not manufacture client work.

Shared headings and closing CTAs have stronger scale and spacing. Industry hero images use a consistent editorial crop with separate readable text. Mobile layouts stack cleanly and preserve touch targets. Hover motion is modest and respects reduced-motion preferences.

The pending industry refinement is included: 20 sector-specific before/after workflow illustrations, generated-image captions removed, three more relevant service photographs, and comparison controls protected against interaction before client initialization.

## Validation

The original homepage image and ProductMockup source remain unchanged. The 51-route HTTP check verifies caption removal and the 20 workflow renders. Regression tests, lint, production compilation, and representative desktop/mobile browser checks are recorded with this implementation. CMS data and submission endpoints are unchanged.
